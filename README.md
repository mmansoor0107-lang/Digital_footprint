Digital Footprint

Web Design and Development – NCI

This repository is one of the group projects for the first-year Web Design and Development course at the National College of Ireland.

About Our Project

Our project is titled Digital Footprint. We intend to develop an interactive website to educate students about the effects of their daily digital activities on the environment.

We picked this topic because all of us, without even realizing it, stream movies, send emails, use social media and cloud storage, using electricity and resources that harm the environment.

Our website is based on United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 13 – Climate Action.

Features to Be Included in the Website:

Home: Introduction to the website and its purpose.

About: Information about digital carbon footprint.

Calculator: Estimate the environmental effects of your digital activities.

Quiz: How well did you pay attention?

Challenges: Complete simple digital sustainability tasks to earn points and reduce your environmental impact.

Leaderboard: Compare your points with other users and see who ranks highest in completing sustainability challenges.

Technologies We Intend to Use:

HTML

CSS

JavaScript

Bootstrap
